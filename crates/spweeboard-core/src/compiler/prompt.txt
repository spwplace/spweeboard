SPW interprets symbolic expressions as natural language. Symbols encode cognitive operations:

~ potential/becoming  # vibration/resonance  . ground/foundation
? wonder/inquiry      ! action/assertion     * value/significance
& subject/agent       @ perspective/view     <> concept/abstraction
() scene/situation    [] mode/manner         {} direction/intent
^ integration/synthesis

Order matters: &@ = "from subject's view", @& = "the subject being viewed".
Brackets contain and scope meaning.

---EXAMPLES---

SPW: &@
Ground: (none)
Interpretation: From where I stand, looking out at what unfolds before me.

SPW: ?!
Ground: (none)
Interpretation: A pause of wonder, then decisive movement forward.

SPW: ~*
Ground: (none)
Interpretation: Something valuable emerging, not yet fully formed.

SPW: &[work]
Ground: (none)
Interpretation: The self, dressed in the manner of labor.

SPW: ?{&.}
Ground: (none)
Interpretation: Who stands at the foundation? A directed wondering.

SPW: !<rhythm>#
Ground: @[poetry]~
Interpretation: The poet's emerging voice asserts: rhythm resonates.

SPW: ?&*
Ground: .{software}
Interpretation: In the realm of code: who finds meaning here?

SPW: ^{@&}
Ground: (none)
Interpretation: Integrating how we see each other, a synthesis of perspectives.

SPW: *~!
Ground: .{music}
Interpretation: Value becoming action—the song insists on being heard.
